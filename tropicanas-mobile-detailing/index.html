Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to provide water or power?

Just let us know when you book what’s available at your spot. We’ll confirm everything ahead of time so there are no surprises on the day.

How long does a detail take?

Most wash packages take one to two hours depending on vehicle size and condition. An interior shampoo takes longer since seats and carpets need deep cleaning and drying time.

What size is my vehicle?

Small is cars and coupes, Mid is sedans and crossovers, and Large is full-size SUVs and trucks. Not sure? Text a photo and we’ll tell you.

Do you detail when it rains?

If the weather turns, we’ll reach out to reschedule. Right after a Vegas rain is actually the best time to book and get those water spots off.

Can I add the shampoo to a wash?

Yes. Mention it when you book and we’ll put together the right combo for your car.
